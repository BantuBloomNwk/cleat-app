# Cleat, dApp Store listing

## Name
Cleat

## Short description (one line)
An agent that trades for you and cannot cross the line you wrote.

## Long description
You write one sentence about how your money should be handled. Something like "moderate growth, nothing over fifteen percent in one name, and no fossil fuels."

That sentence goes on Solana, in an account only your key can change. An AI agent proposes trades in tokenized stocks, and every proposal is checked against your sentence before anything happens. It comes back cleared, trimmed to fit, or refused.

The agent can read your sentence and cannot edit it. The check on how much of a sector you already hold runs without anyone seeing the number, so the agent never learns what you own. Every decision is written to a public log with the reason and the sector, never the company or the amount.

Bring your own model if you like. Claude, OpenAI or Gemini, with your key kept on your phone.

Sign in with a passkey, or with the wallet on your Seeker. Cleat never holds your key.

Cleat runs on Solana devnet today. Nothing here moves real money.
